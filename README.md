# The Two Popes

## About the Movie

**The Two Popes** (2019) is my *favorite movie so far*. It was directed by **Fernando Meirelles** and written by **Anthony McCarten**, exploring an unlikely friendship behind the walls of the Vatican.

> "Change requires compromise."

---

## Why I Love It

1. The **dialogue** is sharp, witty, and deeply thoughtful.
2. The **acting** from Anthony Hopkins and Jonathan Pryce is unforgettable.
3. Both leaders feel genuinely *human* and relatable.

### Things That Stand Out

* Vatican and Castel Gandolfo settings
  * Contrasting traditional and modern perspectives
  * Everyday moments like eating pizza and watching football
* Intimate, realistic cinematography
* Strong themes of forgiveness, faith, and friendship
